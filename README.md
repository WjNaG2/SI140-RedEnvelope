# Inverse Enginneering of Wechat RedEnvelope

This is a final project of 25 fall SI140A course of Shanghaitech University.

The project aims to analyze the distribution of red envelope amounts received by different people in a group chat on WeChat. Our major contributions are listed below:
1. We collected **real-world data** of red envelope distributions from three group chats on WeChat.
2. We proposed a simple yet effective method based on **Diffusion** to generate synthetic red envelope distributions that closely resemble the real-world data.
3. We propose a **Segmented PDF Model** to fit the observed distribution pattern of the amounts.
